# 🚀 KeyGuard Roadmap

This document clearly outlines the phased development, launch, and growth strategy for KeyGuard, the enterprise-grade credential management SaaS platform.

---

## 📅 Phase 1: MVP Launch (Q2 2025)

**Core Objectives:**

- Secure Vault (AES-256 encryption)
- OAuth Token Automation (Google & Slack as initial integrations)
- Basic Audit Logs
- Simple UI Dashboard
- Freemium & Pro subscription tiers
- Basic monitoring

---

## 📅 Phase 2: Early Enterprise Adoption (Q3 2025)

**Core Objectives:**

- Expanded integrations (GitHub, GitLab, Jira, Outlook)
- Single Sign-On (SSO) basic (Okta integration)
- Advanced monitoring & alerting (email, webhooks)
- Enhanced UI/UX
- Enterprise branding & custom integrations capability
- Initial compliance documentation and policies (GDPR, SOC 2)

---

## 📅 Phase 3: Compliance & Security Focus (Q4 2025)

**Core Objectives:**

- Full compliance reporting dashboard
- AI-driven anomaly detection and real-time alerts
- Complete RBAC functionality
- Additional SSO providers (Azure AD)
- Security audits & penetration testing (third-party review)
- Full enterprise subscription tier launch

---

## 📅 Phase 4: Scaling & Market Expansion (Q1-Q2 2026)

**Core Objectives:**

- Scalability optimization (multi-region deployment)
- Expanded hosting options (Fly.io, Railway.app, AWS)
- Community-driven integration library
- Full automation of sales/support via AI-driven chatbot and knowledge base
- Increased market visibility (content marketing, webinars, whitepapers)

---

## 📅 Future Opportunities (Q3 2026 & Beyond)

**Core Objectives:**

- Continuous AI enhancement (predictive analytics, NLP querying of logs)
- On-premise or private cloud offering
- Enterprise training programs & certification
- Strategic partnerships with IDP providers (Okta, Azure, Auth0)
- IPO or strategic acquisition preparation

---

## 📌 Strategic Goals

- Build and maintain enterprise trust through explicit security practices.
- Automate as much of the sales, onboarding, and support processes as possible.
- Keep platform lean, secure, and scalable with heavy reliance on AI-driven tools.
